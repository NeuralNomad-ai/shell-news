Shell Appointed Google UK's 24/7 Carbon-Free Energy Manager for New Hertfordshire Data Centre

Google has selected Shell Energy Europe to manage its renewable power portfolio for a newly opened data centre in Waltham Cross, Hertfordshire — Shell's first formal role as a hyperscaler's 24/7 Carbon-Free Energy Manager in the UK. Shell will optimise Google's offtake from the Moray West offshore wind project (ENGIE), deploying battery energy storage systems (BESS) to balance supply. Google UK is targeting 95% carbon-free energy in 2026 at the site. For Shell, this deal is strategically significant: it converts energy trading & optimisation capabilities into a B2B managed-service product directly targeting the fastest-growing electricity demand category — AI data centres. Advisory angle: as Accenture helps Shell expand its AI-era energy services, this deal validates a new revenue model that could scale across hyperscalers in EU and North America.
