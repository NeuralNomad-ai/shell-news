Shell's Own Energy Transition Strategy Wins 78% Shareholder Mandate — Reduced Near-Term Carbon Targets Ratified

At the May 19 AGM, Shell's board-sponsored energy transition resolution — which includes revised (relaxed) near-term carbon intensity reduction targets — passed with 78% support, while the Paris-alignment resolution (Follow This) was defeated with just 13% backing. The decisive margin gives Sawan a clear institutional mandate to pursue the current strategy: sustaining ~1.4 mb/d liquids through 2030, accelerating LNG, and investing 20–30% of capex in lower-carbon solutions. For Accenture advisors, this settles the near-term strategic framing — conversations with Shell ExCo will centre on execution capability and digital transformation, not strategic direction debates. The post-AGM governance clarity is the single most important context for the next 12–18 months of engagement.
