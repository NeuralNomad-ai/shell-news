Shell's Holland Hydrogen 1 ($1.17B) Connects to Dutch Pipeline — Operational Late 2026

Shell's Rotterdam Hydrogen Company has connected the 200 MW Holland Hydrogen 1 electrolysis plant to Gasunie's 32 km hydrogen pipeline, a milestone ahead of expected late-2026 commercial start. At capacity, the plant will produce 60,000 kg/day of green hydrogen for Shell's Energy and Chemicals Park Rotterdam, displacing grey hydrogen in refinery operations. The project has faced delays but is now tracking to first production before year-end, making it a tangible proof point for Shell's "low carbon solutions to 10% of capital employed by 2030" commitment. Advisory angle: Accenture teams advising Shell on industrial decarbonisation and digital operations can use this as a reference case for agentic AI in electrolyser performance monitoring.
