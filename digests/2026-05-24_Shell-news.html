Shell Tests Market for $3B+ North West Shelf Australia Stake — XRG and Midocean Among Bidders

Holland Hydrogen 1 Connected to Dutch Pipeline Network — Europe's Largest Green H₂ Plant Eyes Late-2026 First Production

Shell's 200MW Holland Hydrogen 1 electrolyser at Rotterdam Maasvlakte 2 has been connected to Gasunie's 32km hydrogen pipeline, the key infrastructure milestone before first gas. At full ramp-up in 2027, the €1B+ facility will produce 60 tonnes/day of green hydrogen, replacing grey hydrogen at Shell's adjacent Pernis refinery. The project received ~€150M in EU subsidy support. This is Shell's most tangible near-term proof point for its energy transition commitments — directly relevant to the 78% AGM endorsement of its energy transition strategy and the 13% Follow This vote (down from 30% in 2021) that showed institutional patience with incremental progress. For Accenture: this is the type of concrete transition deliverable that strengthens Shell's regulatory and investor credibility narrative.

Shell and GE Vernova Sign LNG Decarbonization Development Agreement — Hydrogen Co-Firing to Reduce Facility Scope 1 Emissions

Shell and GE Vernova's Gas Power division have signed a Development Agreement to co-develop a decarbonization pathway for LNG liquefaction facilities via hydrogen co-firing in gas turbines. The collaboration directly targets Scope 1 emissions from LNG plants — a growing pressure point as the EU methane regulation (EU MR) tightens requirements on imported LNG carbon intensity. Strategic read: with LNG Canada Phase 2 expansion now the prize behind the ARC Montney acquisition, Shell is pre-positioning its Canadian LNG assets as lower-carbon-intensity supply for Asian utilities (Japan, South Korea, Taiwan) that face domestic net-zero mandates. The GE Vernova partnership provides technology credibility for that commercial differentiation argument — expect this to appear in LNG Canada Phase 2 financing and offtake negotiations.
