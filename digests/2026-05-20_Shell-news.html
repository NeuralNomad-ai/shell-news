ARC Resources Acquisition: Regulatory Path to H2 2026 Close — Montney Combined Position Now ~2M Net Acres

Shell confirmed at Q1 results that its C$22B acquisition of ARC Resources remains on track for H2 2026 completion, subject to Canadian regulatory review and ARC shareholder approval. The deal — ARC shareholders receive 0.40247 Shell shares plus C$8.20 cash per share (C$32.80 total) — combines ARC's 1.5M+ net acres with Shell's ~440K net acres in the Montney formation and adds ~2 billion boe proved plus probable reserves (end-2025). The transaction raises Shell's production CAGR from 1% to ~4% vs 2025, sustaining ~1.4 million bbl/day liquids through 2030. Integrated Gas is the primary beneficiary segment. Advisory angle: AGM clearance and strong shareholder endorsement of Sawan (98.86%) remove one governance risk to deal completion. The next milestones to track are Canadian Competition Bureau review and ARC shareholder vote, likely Q3 2026. Accenture teams advising on Shell's digital and operational integration should begin scoping Montney asset-readiness assessments.
