Shell to Acquire ARC Resources for $16.4B, Adding 370k boe/d from Montney

Shell has agreed to acquire all of ARC Resources' issued shares in a cash-and-stock transaction valued at ~$22B including assumed net debt ($32.80/share; 27% premium to April 24 close). ARC's Montney basin assets (BC and Alberta) add approximately 370,000 boe/d to Shell's portfolio and are projected to deliver 4% production CAGR through 2030. ARC's board unanimously approved and recommends shareholders vote FOR at a special meeting expected in July 2026. Regulatory and court approvals required; close expected H2 2026. This deal reverses Shell's earlier exit from Canadian upstream and signals a decisive doubling-down on integrated gas and LNG feedstock — a direct input into Shell's long-term LNG commercial strategy relevant to the Accenture engagement.

Follow This Resolution 23: Shareholders to Vote on IEA-Scenario Climate Disclosure

Resolution 23, co-filed by Follow This and 23 institutional investors (Belgium, France, Netherlands, UK, US, Switzerland) holding ~0.3% of issued shares, requests Shell publish a report on shareholder value creation under declining oil and gas demand — specifically the IEA's Stated Policies Scenario (STEPS) and Announced Pledges Scenario (APS). Requested disclosures include capex, production and sales by energy source, and free cash-flow projections over Shell's 10-year planning horizon. As a special resolution it needs 75% support to pass. Shell's board unanimously opposes it, calling existing disclosures sufficient. The vote outcome tomorrow will be a leading indicator of institutional investor climate conviction — critical context for advising Shell's C-suite on ESG narrative and investor relations positioning into H2 2026.

Shell published the third edition of its Energy Security Scenarios report, deploying predictive modelling to map three distinct pathways for the global energy system to 2100: Archipelagos (fragmented geopolitics, slower transition), Surge (accelerated clean energy deployment), and Horizon (balanced transition with energy security prioritised).
