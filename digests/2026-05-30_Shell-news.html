LNG Canada Phase 2: BC/Ottawa Investment Pact Signed, Year-End FID Now Formally Targeted

Canada's federal government and British Columbia signed an enhanced investment co-operation agreement with the LNG Canada JV to clear residual commercial and fiscal barriers ahead of a potential Final Investment Decision by end-2026. Shell-INEOS Appomattox Tieback JV: Gulf of America Upstream Consolidation Advances

Lower exploration capex and shared development costs improve the risk-return profile for investors and analysts tracking upstream FCF.

Milieudefensie / Van Doorne · 2026-05-22
Energy-Transition C-suite High Impact Context

Dutch Supreme Court Hears Shell Pleadings: No Individual CO2 Obligation, Ruling Due Autumn 2026

The Supreme Court of the Netherlands held oral arguments in Milieudefensie v. Shell (case 25/00497) on May 22, with the Attorney General's opinion and final ruling expected in autumn 2026 at the earliest. Shell's pleading note argues that the December 2024 Court of Appeal ruling correctly determined that no specific, judicially enforceable emission reduction obligation can be derived from general societal duty-of-care principles for an individual company. Milieudefensie seeks reinstatement of the 2021 district court order (45% Scope 1–3 reduction by 2030 vs. 2019). This is the first time any country's Supreme Court has considered a company's civil law climate obligation — the outcome will set global precedent for corporate climate liability. Watch date: autumn 2026.
