LNG Canada Phase 2 FID Pathway Firms Up: Governments Back Deal, Fluor & JGC Get Green Light to Proceed

Multiple converging signals this week confirm that a Phase 2 Final Investment Decision for LNG Canada is now the base case for year-end 2026. The federal government and Province of BC formalised an "enhanced investment co-operation" agreement in May 2026 to close outstanding commercial, regulatory, and First Nations items. LNG Canada's JV participants (Shell 40%, PETRONAS, PetroChina, Mitsubishi, Korea Gas) then approved hundreds of millions in incremental funding and issued a "limited notice to proceed" to lead contractors Fluor and JGC, allowing preliminary engineering and early procurement to begin ahead of FID. If taken, Phase 2 doubles total facility capacity from 14 to 28 mtpa — making LNG Canada one of the largest LNG export facilities in the world. CEO Sawan has repeatedly described LNG Canada as "transformative," citing surging AI data-centre electricity demand in Asia as the incremental offtake driver. The ARC Resources acquisition (Montney gas) is the feed-gas backstop that makes Phase 2 commercially viable — both decisions are now interdependent on the H2 2026 timeline.
