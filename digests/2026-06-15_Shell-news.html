Shell Selects Aditya Birla Group as Preferred Buyer for Sprng Energy — $1.7B+ Deal

Shell has selected Aditya Birla Group as its preferred buyer for Sprng Energy, the India-based renewable power platform, at a valuation exceeding $1.7 billion. KKR was the runner-up; a consortium of NIIF and Temasek also bid. Sprng was acquired by Shell in 2022 for $1.55 billion and operates solar, wind, and hybrid assets across five Indian states (~3GW portfolio). The ~$1.7B+ exit generates proceeds that directly support ARC Resources acquisition financing ($16.4B deal, closing H2 2026) and supports buyback resumption post-July 14 vote. Advisory read: Execution confirms Shell's capital recycling thesis — exit renewables at a modest premium to reinvest in LNG/upstream and return capital. Indian regulatory approvals (FIPB/CCI) are the remaining deal risk; monitor for any Competition Commission of India conditions given Aditya Birla's existing energy footprint.

Energy Transition & Sustainability

Bloomberg / Power Technology · 2026-06-12 (not in previous digests)
Energy-Transition C-suite High Impact Direct

Shell Appoints Rothschild & PJT Partners for $1B+ Offshore Wind Farm Sale — Near-Complete Renewables Exit

Shell engaged Rothschild & Co and PJT Partners to sell its remaining offshore wind farm portfolio, a process expected to launch in H2 2026 with a close in 2027 at a value exceeding $1 billion. This follows Shell's prior exits from Atlantic Shores Offshore Wind (Oct 2025, 50% interest to EDF), MarramWind and CampionWind (Scotland), and the concurrent Sprng Energy sale in India. Combined with the $1.7B Sprng proceeds, Shell is completing a full withdrawal from large-scale renewables. Advisory read: Shell's energy transition story has materially pivoted — the narrative to the C-suite is now "natural gas as transition fuel + targeted electrification" rather than wind/solar build-out. This is a significant advisory context shift: engagement conversations should reframe Shell's ESG and Scope 3 positioning accordingly, particularly ahead of the investor day and any analyst briefings post-ARC close.
