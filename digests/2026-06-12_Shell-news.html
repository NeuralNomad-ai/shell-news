Qazinform / Astana Times · 2026-06-09
AI-Digital Both Medium Impact Direct

Shell and Kazakhstan Ministry of Energy Sign Geoscience AI Platform MOU — Soviet-Era Subsurface Data to Be Unified via ML

Shell and Kazakhstan's Ministry of Energy (with the National Geological Service) signed a memorandum of cooperation on June 8–9 to build a national geoscience information platform. The initiative will consolidate and reinterpret geological and geophysical data accumulated during the Soviet era and over 35 years of Kazakh independence, leveraging Shell's advanced analytics tools, AI, and ML to identify new oil and gas reservoirs. Core deliverables include a pilot GIS unifying dispersed datasets, capacity building for Kazakh geoscientists in digital data management and subsurface interpretation, and a long-term exploration partnership. Separately, Shell signed a long-term contract in March 2026 for hydrocarbon exploration in the Aktobe region. Advisory implication: Shell is exporting its AI/data capabilities as a strategic asset — the same pattern as C3 AI Reliability and SLB Lumi. Accenture's AI + subsurface data management competency positions it as a natural implementation partner in Kazakhstan; worth flagging in account planning for Central Asia energy.
