Shell in Advanced Talks to Exit French Motorway Petrol Network — 60+ Sites, Q3 Deal, €108M Annual Profit

Shell has informed employees and suppliers of its intention to sell its 60+ French highway concession petrol stations — a business that generated €108.5M operating profit in 2025. The sites are operated via concession agreements with motorway operators Vinci, Cofiroute, and ASF; Shell does not own the land. A deal is targeted by Q3 2026 with completion in early 2027. Despite profitability, the divestment aligns with CEO Wael Sawan's doctrine of concentrating capital on higher-return upstream and LNG assets. This is the latest in a pattern of downstream exits (Shell sold ~800 service stations globally since 2023) that is hollowing out the retail footprint. Advisory context: Shell's downstream transformation creates opportunities in supply chain digitalisation and energy retail platform consulting for whoever acquires these assets.

The failure of even that modest ask signals that majority investors now back Sawan's thesis that disciplined oil-and-gas value is compatible with a 2050 net-zero commitment. For the Accenture engagement: Shell's ESG posture will remain commercially-led not activist-led for the foreseeable future; frame sustainability transformation in ROI terms, not compliance terms.
