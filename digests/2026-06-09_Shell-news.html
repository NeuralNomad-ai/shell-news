Shell 2026 Energy Security Scenarios: Three Pathways — Archipelagos, Surge, Horizon — to 2100

Shell published the third edition of its Energy Security Scenarios, modelling three long-run futures to 2100: Archipelagos (geopolitical fragmentation slows coordination; slower, uneven transition), Surge (technology-led acceleration with massive grid investment and electrification dominance), and Horizon (balanced policy and market forces converge on a managed net-zero path). Shell uses these scenarios internally to stress-test capital allocation — they are not predictions but structured decision tools for the ExCo. The Surge scenario appears most consistent with Shell's current portfolio bias toward LNG and low-carbon power. Accenture advisory implication: Any multi-year digital or operational transformation roadmap presented to Shell leadership should be explicitly scenario-aligned — recommend anchoring base case to Surge while building optionality for Archipelagos contingencies.
