ARC Resources Acquisition: July Shareholder Vote Set, Five Regulatory Hurdles Define H2 Close Timeline

Dutch Supreme Court Post-Hearing: What an Autumn 2026 Ruling Against Shell Could Actually Require

Oral arguments concluded at the Hoge Raad on May 22, 2026. A ruling is expected in autumn 2026 at the earliest. The case trajectory: 2021 District Court ordered Shell to cut Scope 3 emissions 45% by 2030 vs 2019. The 2024 Court of Appeal upheld Shell's general duty of care on climate but struck down the specific percentage obligation, ruling courts cannot mandate exact targets. Milieudefensie is asking the Supreme Court to reinstate the percentage mandate. A Shell loss would create a court-enforced Scope 3 reduction obligation enforceable under Dutch private law — structurally different from AGM resolutions or voluntary commitments, and potentially precedent-setting across European jurisdictions. A Shell win consolidates the 2024 precedent: duty-of-care exists, but courts cannot prescribe specific numbers. Advisory angle: Shell's scenario planning documentation and energy transition disclosure materials — already stress-tested in the 2024 appeal — are directly relevant to Accenture's ESG strategy workstreams.
